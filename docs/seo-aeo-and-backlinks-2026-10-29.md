## Cannibalization checks

- Novelty and patentability are synonyms for one service-section owner; do not split them into competing pages.
- FTO and clearance belong to the FTO section. Its commercial intent differs from the informational comparison article, which should link to that service.
- Landscape and whitespace analysis: service owns commissioned analysis; the existing whitespace article explains a method. Verify actual query-page overlap with Search Console before making consolidation decisions.
- EoU and claim charts belong to the same section within licensing. Portfolio mining covers selection of assets, a distinct step.
- Competitor watch owns ongoing filing/status alerts; competitive intelligence owns interpretation of broader activity.
- Toxicology literature search is the scoped evidence service; the rapid-tox article is an informational guide.
- The brief template is a practical supporting resource linked from the commercial search page, not a duplicate landing page. It remains outside the sitemap and llms.txt primary services list.

Similarity checks are based on source content and intent. Actual competing ranking URLs cannot be diagnosed without query-page data.
